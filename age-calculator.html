Free Age Calculator

Find the calendar age between a date of birth and a chosen reference date. View the result in years, months and days, with total months, weeks and days.

Open Age Calculator →

How age is calculated

The calculator compares the birth date with the selected calculation date, accounting for calendar month lengths and leap years. It also calculates the total number of elapsed days and derives total weeks and months.

How to use this age calculator

Enter your date of birth and the date on which you want to calculate your age. The calculation date defaults to today. Select Calculate age to see the calendar difference and total day, week and month counts.

Example calculation

If a person was born on 15 January 2000 and the reference date is 15 January 2025, the calendar age is 25 years, 0 months and 0 days. Change either date to calculate a different age.

Important limitations

Age is calculated using the dates entered and the device's local current date for the default reference date. Check date formats carefully. The result is not an identity, eligibility or legal-age verification.

Frequently asked questions

Can I calculate age on a past date?

Yes. Enter any reference date that is on or after the date of birth.

Does it account for leap years?

Yes. Calendar date arithmetic and elapsed days account for leap years.

Can I calculate age in total days?

Yes. The result includes total elapsed days and total weeks.
