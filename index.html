WHY CALCPRO

Clarity for everyday calculations

From planning a loan to checking a sale price, Prime CalcPro brings practical calculators together in one convenient place.

Understand your EMI

Estimate a monthly loan payment using the principal, annual interest rate and repayment period. Review the estimated interest and total repayment before comparing loan options.

Work out GST

Calculate an estimated tax amount by adding GST to a base price or extracting GST from a tax-inclusive amount. Always confirm the correct rate for your product or service.

Explore SIP scenarios

See how regular monthly investments may grow over time under an assumed rate of return. Actual investment performance varies and is never guaranteed.

Calculate your age

Use your date of birth and a chosen reference date to estimate your age in years, months, weeks and days. Check that both dates are entered correctly for a useful result.

Check percentages

Find a percentage of a value or calculate what share one number represents of another. These calculations can help with everyday comparisons, budgets and basic number checks.

Compare discounts

Enter an original price and discount rate to estimate the sale price and amount saved. Check the final price against any taxes or additional charges that may apply.

Check your inputs
