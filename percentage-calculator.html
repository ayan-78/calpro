Percentage formulas

To find X% of Y, multiply Y by X and divide by 100. To find what percentage a part is of a whole, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. The whole must not be zero for the second calculation.

How to use this percentage calculator

Enter X and Y to calculate X% of Y. You can also enter an optional part and total to calculate the part as a percentage of the total. Select Calculate percentage to view both results.

Example calculation

20% of 500 is 100. If a part is 100 and the total is 500, the part represents 20% of the total.

Important limitations

The calculator performs basic percentage arithmetic. It does not determine tax rates, investment returns, percentage-point changes or other specialist interpretations unless the values are framed using the displayed formulas.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find 20% of 500?

Multiply 500 by 20 and divide by 100. The result is 100.

How do I find what percentage 25 is of 200?

Divide 25 by 200 and multiply by 100. The result is 12.5%.

Can the total be zero?

No. A percentage-of-total calculation requires a non-zero total.
