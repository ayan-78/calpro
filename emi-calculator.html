How an EMI is calculated

For a standard reducing-balance loan with a fixed interest rate, the monthly instalment is calculated using the principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments. The formula is EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments.

How to use this EMI calculator

Enter the loan principal in rupees, the annual interest rate as a percentage and the tenure in years. Select Calculate EMI to view the estimated monthly payment, principal, interest and total repayment. Try different rates or tenures to compare scenarios.

Example calculation

For illustration, a ₹10,00,000 loan at 8.5% annual interest over 10 years produces an estimated EMI of about ₹12,398 per month. Actual lender figures may differ because of fees, rate resets, payment dates and rounding.

Important limitations

This tool provides an estimate for a fixed-rate reducing-balance loan. It does not include processing fees, insurance, floating-rate changes, prepayments or lender-specific rules. Confirm the repayment schedule with your lender before making a borrowing decision.

Frequently asked questions

Does a longer loan tenure reduce EMI?

A longer tenure usually reduces the monthly instalment but can increase the total interest paid.

Does this calculator include bank fees?

No. Fees, insurance and other lender charges are not included.

Can I use it for a zero-interest loan?

Yes. The calculator handles a 0% annual interest rate as principal divided by the number of months.
