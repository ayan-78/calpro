How a SIP estimate is calculated

This calculator estimates the future value of regular month-end contributions using monthly compounding. It converts the assumed annual return into a monthly rate and applies the future-value formula for a series of equal contributions.

How to use this SIP calculator

Enter the monthly investment, assumed annual return percentage and investment period in years. Select Calculate returns to see the estimated future value, total amount invested and illustrative returns.

Example calculation

For example, investing ₹5,000 at the end of each month for 10 years at an assumed 12% annual return produces an estimated future value of about ₹11,50,193. The total contributions are ₹6,00,000 and the illustrative growth is about ₹5,50,193. This is a mathematical projection, not a promised return; actual outcomes depend on market performance, costs, timing and the return earned.

Investment risk and limitations

This calculator is an educational illustration, not a prediction or guarantee. Mutual fund returns are market-linked and can be negative. The estimate does not account for taxes, expense ratios, exit loads, changing contributions or actual market volatility. Consider scheme documents and qualified financial advice.

Frequently asked questions

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. SIPs are a way to invest regularly; mutual fund returns are market-linked and not guaranteed.

Does the calculator assume monthly investments?

Yes. It assumes equal monthly contributions at the end of each month.

Does it include taxes and fund expenses?

No. Taxes, expense ratios and other charges are not included in the estimate.
