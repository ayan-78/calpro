Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CalcPro ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit https://primecalpro.vercel.app/ and use our online calculators. It covers the analytics and advertising services currently configured on the website.

1. Information you enter into calculators

CalcPro's calculator functions process the values you enter in your browser to produce a result. The calculator functions are designed to perform these calculations locally and do not intentionally send the amounts, rates or dates you enter to a CalcPro application server. Please avoid entering information that personally identifies you.

2. Analytics information

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by Google, to understand how visitors use CalcPro and to improve the website. Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from network information, browser and device details, referral information, interactions and usage events.

CalcPro also sends limited interaction events to GA4 when a visitor selects a calculator or clicks a calculator's calculate button. These events identify the calculator (EMI, GST, SIP, Age, Percentage or Discount); they are not designed to include the values entered into calculator fields.

Google may use cookies or similar technologies to provide analytics and measure website usage. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Advertising and cookies

CalcPro uses Google AdSense advertising services. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve, measure and improve advertisements, limit repeated ads, detect invalid activity and, where permitted, personalize ads based on visits to this or other websites.

Advertising cookies and technologies may be used by Google or third-party vendors. You can learn about Google's advertising practices at Google's advertising technologies page and manage available personalization choices at Google Ads Settings. Your browser also provides controls to block or delete cookies. Blocking cookies may affect some website or advertising features.

4. Hosting and technical information

CalcPro is hosted using Vercel. When you access a website, the hosting and network providers may process technical information such as IP address, request time, browser information and server or security logs to deliver the website, maintain reliability, diagnose errors and protect the service. We do not control the independent privacy practices of these providers. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

5. Information you send us

CalcPro does not currently provide a website contact form. If you email us at calpro@gmail.com, we will receive the email address and the information you choose to include so that we can respond and handle your request.

6. How information is used and shared

Information handled through the services described above may be used to operate and secure CalcPro, understand site usage, measure advertising, prevent abuse, respond to messages and comply with legal obligations. Google and Vercel process information under their own terms and privacy policies when providing their services. We do not intentionally include calculator input values in the custom GA4 events described in this policy.

7. Your choices

You can manage cookies through your browser settings and manage Google's available advertising personalization settings through Google Ads Settings. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information. To make a privacy request, email calpro@gmail.com.

8. Children's privacy

CalcPro is a general-purpose calculator website and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when the policy was most recently revised. Please review this page periodically.

10. Contact us

For questions or privacy requests about this policy, contact CalcPro at calpro@gmail.com.
