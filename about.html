ABOUT CALCPRO

Making numbers easier to understand.

Prime CalPro brings practical everyday calculators into one simple, accessible place.

Our purpose

Prime CalPro brings together six everyday tools: loan EMI, GST, SIP projection, age, percentage and discount calculators. The aim is to make common calculations easier to follow by showing the inputs, the result and an explanation of the assumptions involved.

How to use the tools

Choose the calculator that matches the question you want to answer. Read the labels and enter values in the units requested. Review the result note and the relevant guide to understand what is included and what is not. If you are comparing scenarios, change one input at a time so you can see how that assumption affects the result.

Calculation methods and assumptions

Each tool applies a mathematical method suited to its purpose. The EMI tool estimates a fixed-rate reducing-balance repayment; the GST tool performs add-tax or extract-tax arithmetic; the SIP tool projects regular monthly contributions using an assumed return; the age tool compares calendar dates; the percentage tool performs basic percentage arithmetic; and the discount tool calculates a single percentage reduction. The guides explain the formulas and provide worked examples.

Accuracy and limitations

Prime CalPro provides general-purpose estimates, not personalised financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Real-world amounts may differ because of fees, rounding, tax classifications, changing rates, investment performance, dates and provider-specific rules. Always verify important figures with the relevant bank, government authority, investment provider, seller or qualified professional.

Corrections and suggestions

If you notice a calculation issue, unclear explanation or accessibility problem, please send the page address and a description of the issue through our Contact page. Including the inputs that produced an unexpected result can help us understand and reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal data.
