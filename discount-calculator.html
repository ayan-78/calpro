Free Discount Calculator

Find the price after a percentage discount and see how much you save. Enter the original price and discount rate to calculate an estimated sale price.

Open Discount Calculator →

How discount calculation works

The saving is the original price multiplied by the discount percentage and divided by 100. The sale price is the original price minus the saving.

How to use this discount calculator

Enter the original price in rupees and the discount percentage from 0 to 100. Select Calculate discount to view the final price, amount saved, original price and discount rate.

Example calculation

For an original price of ₹2,500 and a 20% discount, the saving is ₹500 and the sale price is ₹2,000 before any additional charges.

Important limitations

The calculator does not include taxes, shipping, handling, coupon stacking, cashback or store-specific conditions. Check the seller's final checkout price and offer terms.

Frequently asked questions

How much do I save on ₹2,500 at 20% off?

You save ₹500, so the price after discount is ₹2,000 before additional charges.

Can I calculate a 100% discount?

Yes. A 100% discount produces a calculated sale price of zero.

Does it include GST or shipping?

No. Additional taxes and charges are not included.
