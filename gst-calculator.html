How GST calculation works

To add GST, multiply the base amount by the GST rate and add the tax to the base. To extract GST from a tax-inclusive total, divide the total by (1 + rate ÷ 100); the GST component is the difference between the total and the base amount.

How to use this GST calculator

Choose Add GST or Remove GST. Enter the amount, select a GST rate, or choose Custom and enter a rate. Select Calculate GST to see the base amount, tax amount and resulting amount.

Example calculation

If the base price is ₹1,000 and the GST rate is 18%, the GST amount is ₹180 and the total is ₹1,180. If ₹1,180 already includes 18% GST, the pre-tax value is ₹1,000 and the included GST is ₹180.

Important limitations

GST treatment and applicable rates depend on the goods or services, transaction and current rules. This calculator is a general arithmetic aid and does not determine tax classification, exemptions, input tax credit or filing obligations. Verify the applicable rate with official guidance or a tax professional.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove GST from a total price?

Yes. Choose Remove GST and enter the amount that already includes tax.

Which GST rates are available?

The tool offers common preset rates and a custom-rate option. Confirm the rate applicable to your transaction.

Does this calculate CGST and SGST separately?

It calculates the combined GST amount; it does not split the tax into components.
